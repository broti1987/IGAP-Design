What We do

IGAP works on consequential questions where government, regulation and markets converge

We combine rigorous research, institutional insight and sector expertise to anticipate change, clarify choices and help organisations and public institutions move from judgement to action.

Research & Policy Advocacy

Build evidence that can withstand scrutiny

IGAP undertakes independent and commissioned research on policy, regulatory, economic and institutional questions.

We combine legal and policy analysis, economics, data, sectoral expertise and comparative experience to test assumptions, build evidence and develop policy options. Our work informs policy development, institutional decision-making, consultation processes and research-backed advocacy.

Independent research also allows us to pursue questions beyond immediate mandates, build long-term expertise and contribute to issues of wider public importance.

  • Reports and policy briefs
  • Sector studies
  • Consultation responses
  • Evidence building
  • Policy options and design
  • Regulatory impact analysis
  • Economic analysis
  • Research-backed advocacy
  • Primers and explainers
02

Policy Insight & Regulatory Strategy

Understand change early. Respond with judgement

We help organisations and public institutions identify emerging policy and regulatory issues, understand how laws, regulations, standards and institutional priorities are evolving, and assess what those changes mean in practice.

Our work looks beyond formal rules to the political economy, implementation trends, enforcement priorities and institutional considerations that shape how policy and regulation develop and operate.

We help translate that understanding into regulatory approaches, policy positions and strategies grounded in evidence and institutional realities.

  • Policy monitoring and early issue-spotting
  • Regulatory strategy
  • Legislative and policy analysis
  • Consultation strategy
  • Standards and regulatory positioning
  • Union and state policy mapping
  • Implementation and enforcement analysis
  • Political economy analysis
  • Policy scenario analysis
03

Government Engagement & Institutional Strategy

Understand institutions, not just stakeholders

IGAP works both with public institutions and with organisations engaging government, regulators and the wider policy ecosystem. For companies and industry bodies, we help identify where authority sits, how decisions are made, which institutional priorities matter and how to engage constructively around substantive policy questions.

For governments and public institutions, we support policy development, implementation, stakeholder consultation, institutional strategy and the strengthening of policy and regulatory capabilities.

Our approach reflects the reality that decision-making often spans Union and state governments, regulators, public bodies and wider policy communities rather than a single institution.

  • Government engagement strategy
  • Institutional and stakeholder mapping
  • Policy briefings
  • Regulator engagement
  • Union and state government strategy
  • Advisory to public institutions
  • Policy design and implementation
  • Stakeholder consultations
  • Institutional capability building and training
04

Policy Communications, Issue Management & Thought Leadership

Shape the argument, not just the message

Policy outcomes can depend not only on the substance of an issue, but on how it is framed, evidenced and understood by government, industry, experts, media and public audiences.

IGAP helps organisations and institutions develop clear, credible policy arguments grounded in evidence and institutional context. We translate complex legal, regulatory and policy questions into narratives and materials suited to the audiences that matter.

We also support thought leadership and sensitive issue management where public policy, public interest, reputation and institutional trust intersect.

  • Policy narratives
  • Issue management
  • Thought leadership
  • Public-interest framing
  • Briefing materials
  • Speeches and op-eds
  • Consultation communications
  • White papers and explainers
  • Convenings
  • Reputation-sensitive policy matters
05

Market, Investment & Ecosystem Strategy

See the market in the context that shapes it

IGAP supports organisations assessing, entering, investing in and expanding across India.

We examine commercial opportunity alongside regulation, political economy, public policy, state priorities, institutional structures and wider economic and geopolitical developments. This helps identify not only where opportunities exist, but the conditions that will shape whether a market or investment strategy can work in practice.

Our work spans market access and entry, national and state-level opportunities, investment strategy, partnerships and ecosystem development, including how these should be sequenced in practice.

  • Market assessment
  • India entry and expansion
  • Trade and market access strategy
  • Investment strategy
  • State-level opportunity analysis
  • Partner and investor mapping
  • Ecosystem strategy
  • Market sequencing
  • Policy and regulatory mapping
  • Institutional strategy
06

Coalitions, Partnerships & Industry Platforms

Some policy questions are better advanced collectively

IGAP helps design, convene and support coalitions, partnerships and industry platforms around shared policy, sectoral and public-interest priorities.

This can involve bringing together companies, industry bodies, experts, academic institutions, think tanks, civil society organisations and other stakeholders around a defined agenda.

We support the research, common agenda, coordination and engagement required to build effective platforms, develop shared positions, strengthen the evidence around an issue and sustain constructive engagement over time.

  • Coalition strategy
  • Industry platforms
  • Association strategy and support
  • Working groups
  • Member coordination
  • Common policy agendas
  • Expert networks
  • Academic, think-tank and civil society partnerships
  • Convenings
  • Partnership development

Who We Work With

IGAP works with organisations across the public, private, research and development ecosystems.

  • Companies and founders

    Multilateral institutions, development organisations and other international bodies engaging with India’s policy, institutional and economic landscape.
