Areas of Focus

IGAP works across a broad range of policy, regulatory and strategic domains.

These areas illustrate the domains in which we work rather than define their limits. Much of our work cuts across them, reflecting the ways in which technology, markets, institutions and public priorities increasingly intersect.

01Technology, AI & Digital Economy 02Connectivity & Digital Infrastructure 03Industrial Policy, Trade & Investment 04Competition, Consumer & Market Regulation 05National Security, Defence & Strategic Technologies 06Energy, Climate & Sustainability 07Governance, Institutions & Public Sector Reform 08Justice, Social Policy & Human Development

01 Technology, AI & Digital Economy

We work on the policy, regulatory and institutional questions arising from technological change and the growth of the digital economy, including how emerging technologies are governed, adopted and integrated into markets and public systems.

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Emerging technologies
  • Data governance and privacy
  • Digital platforms
  • Cybersecurity
  • Fintech
  • Interactive entertainment
  • Digital-market regulation

02 Connectivity & Digital Infrastructure

We work on the infrastructure, investment and policy frameworks that underpin digital capacity and connectivity, from telecommunications and spectrum to compute, data centres and digital public infrastructure.

Our work considers regulation, market structure, infrastructure development and the relationship between digital capacity and wider economic and strategic priorities.

  • Telecommunications
  • Spectrum
  • Broadband and connectivity
  • Data centres
  • Cloud and compute infrastructure
  • Digital public infrastructure
  • Infrastructure policy and investment

03 Industrial Policy, Trade & Investment

We examine how governments build productive capacity, attract investment, strengthen supply chains and shape the development of industries and markets.

Our work spans industrial and manufacturing policy, trade and market access, investment frameworks and the increasingly important relationship between economic policy, technology, resilience and national capability.

  • Industrial strategy
  • Advanced manufacturing
  • Semiconductors and electronics
  • Mobility and electric vehicles
  • Agri-food supply chains
  • Trade, tariffs and market access
  • Investment and FDI policy
  • Industrial incentives and state-level development

04 Competition, Consumer & Market Regulation

We work on the rules and institutions that shape competition, consumer markets and regulated industries.

This includes questions of market structure, competition policy, product and consumer regulation, taxation, advertising, financial regulation and the obligations faced by businesses operating in markets subject to significant public and regulatory scrutiny.

  • Competition and antitrust
  • Consumer and product regulation
  • Financial services
  • Healthcare and life sciences regulation
  • Taxation and excise
  • Advertising and consumer protection
  • Food and consumer goods
  • Market conduct and sectoral regulation

05 National Security, Defence & Strategic Technologies

We work at the intersection of national security, technology, industrial capability and economic policy.

Our work examines how states build and protect strategic capability, respond to technological and geopolitical change, strengthen resilience and develop the industrial and institutional foundations required for national security.

  • Defence and security policy
  • Aerospace, aviation and space
  • Drones and autonomous systems
  • Defence-industrial policy
  • Critical and emerging technologies
  • Economic security and resilience
  • Sovereign capability
  • Critical infrastructure and strategic supply chains

06 Energy, Climate & Sustainability

We work on policy, regulatory and investment questions arising from the energy transition and the growing integration of climate and sustainability into economic and industrial policy.

Our work considers emerging markets and technologies as well as the regulatory, institutional and commercial implications of transition for existing industries.

  • Energy transition
  • Climate policy
  • Renewable and emerging energy
  • Sustainable infrastructure
  • Sustainability regulation
  • ESG and responsible business
  • Green industry
  • Carbon markets
  • Resource efficiency

07 Governance, Institutions & Public Sector Reform

We examine how public institutions are designed, how state capacity is built and how policy moves from formal rules to implementation.

Our work can support governments and public institutions on institutional design, administrative and regulatory reform, capability building and the systems through which public policy is developed and delivered.

  • State capacity
  • Institutional design
  • Administrative and regulatory reform
  • Public finance
  • Policy implementation
  • Centre-state and federal governance
  • Institutional capability building
  • Digital government and public systems

08 Justice, Social Policy & Human Development

We work on policy questions affecting rights, opportunity, access to services and human development.

Our work can span justice, health, education, inclusion and social protection, including the ways in which technology and institutional change affect access, delivery and outcomes.

  • Access to justice
  • Technology and justice
  • Health and mental health
  • Education and skills
  • Social protection
  • Inclusion and accessibility
  • Public service delivery
  • Rights and accountability
