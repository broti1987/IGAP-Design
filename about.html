The IGAP Approach

The Indian Governance and Policy Project (IGAP) is a research led policy and strategic advisory organisation working across public policy, governance, markets, geopolitics and strategic affairs.

We approach public policy not only as a question of rules and regulation, but also of institutions, markets, state capacity and political economy. Our work examines how these interact with India’s developmental priorities and with a changing economic, technological and geopolitical environment.

IGAP brings together legal, policy, economic and sectoral expertise to understand complex questions and develop approaches that are rigorous, contextually grounded and workable in practice.

What Shapes Our Work

India is building markets and institutions together

In many sectors, India is not simply regulating mature markets. It is building capacity, setting rules, attracting investment, creating institutions and developing domestic capability while pursuing wider economic, developmental and strategic objectives.

Policy therefore cannot be understood in isolation from markets, state capacity, political economy or the wider geopolitical environment. We look at these together.

Research should have consequences

Good research should improve decisions, test assumptions, sharpen public debate and strengthen the evidence on which policy and institutional choices are made.

It should also help identify important questions before they become urgent policy problems.

Strategy must survive contact with institutions

Policy does not operate only on paper, and strategy cannot be developed in isolation from how institutions actually work.

We pay attention to where authority sits, how governments and regulators make decisions, what institutions are capable of doing, how policy is implemented across the Union and the states, and what execution requires in practice.

A policy that cannot survive institutional reality is not yet a workable policy.

India needs answers grounded in Indian realities

Comparative experience matters. But policy frameworks cannot simply be transplanted across jurisdictions.

India’s developmental priorities, federal structure, political economy, institutional capacity, market structure and strategic environment shape both the challenges it faces and the approaches most likely to work.

Our work is grounded in those realities while remaining informed by global experience.

National ambition and constitutional values belong together

IGAP works to advance legitimate national security, economic, developmental and governance priorities while remaining firmly anchored in constitutional values, the rule of law and institutional accountability.

Durable public policy requires both the capacity to pursue national priorities and the institutions, safeguards and accountability that give public action legitimacy.

From
Research

Complex policy questions rarely yield useful answers through research alone or strategy alone.

Together, they help us identify what matters, what is feasible, where trade-offs lie and what effective action will require.

Research builds evidence, tests assumptions and develops long-term expertise. Advisory tests those ideas against institutional realities, market dynamics and implementation challenges.

To
Action

Together, they allow us to move from analysis to judgement, and from judgement to action.

From

Each disciplines the other.

Research keeps our advisory work evidence-led and attentive to longer-term questions. Practical engagement keeps our research grounded in how institutions and markets actually work.

Research and advisory strengthen each other

Independent research gives us space to pursue questions beyond an immediate mandate and contribute to issues of wider public importance. Advisory brings us closer to the realities of decision-making and implementation.
